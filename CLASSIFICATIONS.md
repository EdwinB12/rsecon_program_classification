1. Software: A talk about a **general-purpose or cross-cutting** tool, technique, platform, framework, or software-engineering practice. The subject applies across research domains — testing, CI/CD, packaging, observability, cloud and HPC platforms, generic ML/AI infrastructure, reproducibility tooling, sustainability tooling, training platforms. The artifact serves RSEs and researchers as a *population*, not a specific scientific question.

2. Research: A talk about software **built for or applied to a specific research domain**. Two flavours, both qualify:
   - (a) a tool, library, framework, or platform purpose-built for a particular scientific or scholarly need;
   - (b) a workflow showing how software helps answer a domain research question (results may or may not be presented).

   The acid test: if you can't explain the talk without naming the discipline, it's Research.

3. Community: About **people and the profession**. RSE careers, identity, and recognition; team management; training and curriculum; community-building (SIGs, networks, regional groups); profession-wide initiatives (mental health, diversity, sustainability advocacy); cross-organisation policy and infrastructure (FAIR, OSPOs, JOSS as a recognition venue). Distinguished from Software by not centring a specific tool/technique, and from Research by not serving a specific scientific domain.


4. Other: Doesn't fit into any of the above.
